# Pinnacle Packaging (Pvt) Ltd Website

Official corporate website for **Pinnacle Packaging (Pvt) Ltd**.

A responsive packaging company website built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to showcase company information, products, and services.

---

## About The Company

Pinnacle Packaging (Pvt) Ltd is a packaging solutions manufacturer based in Kadawatha, Sri Lanka.

The company provides:

- Adhesive tapes
- Packaging films
- Industrial packaging solutions
- Custom packaging products

---

- Responsive company website
