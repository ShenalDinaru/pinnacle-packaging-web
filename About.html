OUR STORY

From Trading Company to Modern Manufacturer

Founded in 2022, Pinnacle Packaging (Pvt) Ltd began with a vision of delivering innovative packaging solutions to Sri Lanka's growing export market. What started as a trusted trading company has rapidly evolved into a modern manufacturing business with a state-of-the-art production facility established in Kadawatha in 2025. Today, we proudly serve businesses across multiple industries by providing premium packaging solutions backed by quality, innovation and reliability.

Our commitment to continuous improvement, sustainable manufacturing and customer satisfaction has enabled us to build long-term partnerships while consistently delivering products that meet international standards.
