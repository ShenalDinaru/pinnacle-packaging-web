PINNACLE PACKAGING

Pinnacle Packaging (Pvt) Ltd is a forward-thinking packaging solutions provider committed to delivering innovative, reliable, and high-quality packaging products for diverse industries. Established with a vision to support Sri Lanka’s growing commercial and export sectors, we have grown from a trusted trading company into a modern manufacturer offering a wide range of self-adhesive tapes, films, and customized packaging solutions. With a strong focus on quality, technology, and customer satisfaction, we combine advanced manufacturing practices with sustainable solutions to create packaging that protects products, enhances brand value, and delivers lasting performance.
